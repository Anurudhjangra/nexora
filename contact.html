Common Questions

Before You Ask

How much does a website cost?
It depends on the project — a landing page costs less than a full e-commerce store. Send me your requirements and I'll give you a clear, honest quote before we start. No hidden charges.
How long will my project take?
A landing page usually takes 3–5 days, a business website 1–2 weeks, and a web app or store 2–4 weeks. You'll get a timeline upfront and regular progress updates along the way.
Do you provide revisions?
Yes. Every project includes a set number of revision rounds so we can get the design exactly right. Small tweaks after launch are always welcome.
What if I need changes after the site is live?
No problem. I offer ongoing support and maintenance, so you can keep your site updated and secure even after the project is delivered.
