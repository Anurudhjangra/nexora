My Story

Why I do what I do

I started building websites because I love the process — taking a simple idea and turning it into something people can actually use and feel proud of. What began as a hobby quickly became a freelance career when local businesses started asking me to build their online presence.

Over the last two years I've worked with freelancers, startups and small businesses on everything from single landing pages to full e-commerce stores. Every project taught me the same lesson: a website only works when it is fast, clear and honest — no unnecessary complexity, just design and code that does the job.

Today I run NEXORA as a one-person studio focused on frontend development and web design. I handle everything myself — design, development, testing and delivery — so you get one clear point of contact from start to finish.

When I'm not building, I'm learning new tools, exploring UI trends, or helping other small businesses make their first move online.
